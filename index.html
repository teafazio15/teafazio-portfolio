Technical notes

Wheel assembly: bearings, analysis & manufacturing +

Choosing the bearings

I compared preload, bearing spacing, fatigue life, and total assembly mass in SKF SimPro Quick. Cost and availability ruled out the 71817 option. Early CAD studies helped resolve suspension hardpoints and wheel clearances.

Earlier design review

The presentation records an earlier 2025–26 iteration: 0.219° modeled bearing-bore deflection under a 2-2-3g load, reported as a 15% improvement. Rear assembly mass was 4.3 lb versus 4.26 lb previously. Physical deflection validation was still planned.

The latest result is 26% lower toe and camber compliance with reduced assembly mass.

From drawing to assembly

The 7075-T6 upright used 22 mm bearing spacing, captive nuts, slotted camber shims, and a controlled bearing press fit. The team reached wheels down two months earlier than in previous years.
